## Anti-Goals (What We're NOT Doing)

Explicitly call these out to keep the system honest:

- Not chasing PRs at the cost of asymmetry blowups
- Not extreme cutting or restrictive dieting
- Not replacing the human coach — supplement, don't usurp
- Not replacing PT consultation when needed — flag and defer
- Not over-engineering the system before it's proven its basic value
