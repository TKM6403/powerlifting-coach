# Mobility Protocols

Daily and as-needed mobility work, ordered by purpose.

---

## Daily Stabilizer Routine (~15 min)

Run before training sessions as activation, or on rest days as standalone work.

3 rounds, minimal rest between exercises, 60-90 sec rest between rounds.

1. **Dead bug** — 8 per side. Lower back pressed to floor, opposite arm and leg extend slowly.
2. **Side-lying clamshell with band** — 12 per side. Band above knees, open top knee without rotating hips back.
3. **Glute bridge, single leg** — 10 per side. Don't let hips tilt sideways. Hard squeeze at top.
4. **Bird dog** — 8 per side, 2-sec hold. Hips stay square.
5. **Pallof press with band** — 10 per side. Band anchored at chest height, press straight out, resist rotation.
6. **Copenhagen plank (regressed: knee on bench)** — 20-sec hold per side. Progress to full lever when comfortable.

### Progression
- Week 1-2: nail form, 2 rounds
- Week 3+: 3 rounds
- Then: add reps (8 → 10 → 12), then add band tension, then add hold time on planks
- When 3 rounds at top end feel easy: regress to a harder variation (full-lever Copenhagen, banded bird dog, single-leg dead bug) and start the rep progression over

Track completion in `logs/sessions/` alongside SBD numbers. Stabilizer work has metrics too.

## Pre-Squat / Pre-Deadlift Hip Unlock (5 min)

Use as standalone if the daily routine has already been done that day and the hip just needs a quick unlock.

1. Cat-cow × 8
2. 90/90 hip switches × 6 per side, slow
3. Couch stretch, left × 60-90 sec, with pelvic tuck and glute squeeze
4. Glute bridge × 10
5. Single-leg glute bridge, left planted × 8

## PRI-Style Position Resets (Self-Administered)

These are positional resets, not workouts. They reposition the pelvis and ribcage rather than strengthening anything. Look up exact technique on YouTube ("PRI 90/90 hip lift," "PRI left AIC reset").

**Always do these BEFORE the warmup, not as the warmup.** Reset position first, then strengthen in the new position.

### 90/90 Hip Lift with Balloon (or Full Exhale)
- Lie on back, feet up on a chair or bench, knees and hips at 90/90
- Inhale through nose, exhale fully through mouth (or into balloon)
- At end of exhale, drive heels down into bench, lift hips slightly, hold
- 4-5 reps of 4-5 breaths each

### Left Adductor Pullback
- Side-lying with left side up, top leg (left) on couch/bench
- Pull left knee back into the surface, holding
- 4 sets of 5 deep breaths

### All-Fours Belly Lift
- Quadruped, knees just behind hips
- Round upper back slightly, exhale fully, draw belly up toward spine
- Hold 5 breaths, 4 rounds

## Cooldown / Tissue-Warm Hold (Post-Lift, ~3 min)

Tissue is warm; stretches taken here hold better than cold.

1. Couch stretch, left × 60-90 sec (tuck, squeeze)
2. Open book stretch × 5 per side
3. Cross-body pec minor stretch, left × 30 sec
4. Easy walking 3-5 min if available

---

## Anti-Sitting Habits

Sitting all day undoes mobility work. Throughout the day:
- Every hour: stand up, 10 glute squeezes or a 30-sec couch stretch
- Long meetings: stand or kneel periodically
- Long car/plane: get up, walk, stretch hips and chest

---

## Travel / Minimum-Equipment Routine

Bodyweight version of the daily routine. No band? Substitutions in parens.

1. Dead bug × 8 per side
2. Side-lying leg raise × 12 per side (no band — focus on slow control)
3. Single-leg glute bridge × 10 per side
4. Bird dog × 8 per side, 2-sec hold
5. Side plank with hip dip × 6 per side (substitute for Pallof when no band)
6. Copenhagen plank (knee or full lever) × 20 sec per side

## Self-Assessment Battery (Weekly, ~5 min)

Track these over time. Mobility Agent reads recent results to detect trends.

### Test 1: Standing Forward Hinge Asymmetry
Stand barefoot, feet hip-width, slow forward fold with arms hanging.
- Are both hands at the same height?
- If not, which is lower and by how much (rough estimate)?

### Test 2: Single-Leg Glute Bridge Feel
Single-leg bridge, left planted, then right planted.
- Can you feel the working glute clearly?
- Compare left vs. right — same? Worse left? Improving?

### Test 3: Hip Flexor Stretch Sensation
Half-kneeling stretch, both sides, 30 sec each.
- Where do you feel the stretch (deep front of hip, mid-thigh, knee)?
- Left vs. right comparison

### Test 4: Active Straight Leg Raise
Lie on back, raise one leg ~6" off the floor straight.
- Effort difference between sides?
- Lift height difference?

### Test 5: Trendelenburg
Stand on one leg, lift other foot. Watch pelvis from behind in mirror.
- Does the lifted-side hip drop? (Indicates weak glute med on the standing leg.)

Log results to a section of `logs/patterns/` weekly.

---

## When Self-Management Isn't Enough

If any of the following: book a PT consult.
- Daily routine done consistently for 6+ weeks, no improvement
- New pain, especially sharp or sustained
- Asymmetry worsening rather than improving
- Pattern feels stuck — same flares cycling block after block

Look for: sports/orthopedic PT, ideally PRI-trained or DNS-trained, ideally with experience treating powerlifters.
