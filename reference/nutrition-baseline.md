## Meal Timing Defaults

### Training day
- **Breakfast:** moderate protein + moderate carbs, 2-3 hours before training if possible
- **Pre-workout (if needed):** 30-50g carbs + 20-30g protein, ~1 hour before
- **Intra-workout:** water + electrolytes; carbs optional for sessions > 60 min
- **Post-workout:** within a few hours, hit a substantial protein + carb meal (the carne asada burrito is fine here)
- **Evening:** protein-forward, balance the day's macros

### Rest day
- Same protein target
- Lower carbs OK (drop ~50-100g)
- Same fat
- Calories slightly lower than training day

---

## Hydration

- ~1 oz water per pound of bodyweight daily as a starting point
- Electrolytes if sweating a lot or training in heat (LMNT or homemade equivalent)
- Caffeine before lifting OK; cap around 4 PM to protect sleep

---

## Supplements Worth Considering

Short list. Most supplements don't matter much.

- **Creatine monohydrate** — 5g/day, every day, no loading needed. Best-studied supplement in lifting.
- **Whey protein** — convenience tool for hitting protein targets
- **Caffeine** — pre-workout, 100-300mg as tolerated
- **Vitamin D** — if blood levels are low (test, don't guess)
- **Fish oil** — modest evidence, if diet is low in fatty fish

Everything else is either unnecessary, mildly helpful at best, or marketing.
