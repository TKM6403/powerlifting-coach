# Warmup Library

All warmups customized for tkm's left-side asymmetry pattern. Mobility Agent draws from these and tailors to current state.

**Universal principle:** Stretching alone plateaus. Pair lengthening of tight tissue with activation of opposing structures, then groove the movement pattern, then ramp the bar.

---

## Squat (Heavy / Top Set Day)

~15-20 min.

### Phase 1: Unlock the hip (4-5 min)
1. **Cat-cow** — 8 reps slow
2. **90/90 hip switches** — 6 per side, slow; 5-sec end-range holds if left feels stuck
3. **Couch stretch, left** — 90 sec, tuck pelvis, squeeze left glute
4. **Couch stretch, right** — 30 sec (balance only)
5. **Half-kneeling hip flexor with overhead reach, left** — 45 sec
6. **World's greatest stretch** — 3 per side, slow

### Phase 2: Weighted core + hip flexor work (4-5 min)
Optional progression layer — when ready to move past activation-only.
7. **Goblet squat with 3-sec pause at bottom** — 6 reps, light KB (25-35 lb)
8. **Suitcase carry, left side** — 30 sec walk, moderate weight
9. **Weighted dead bug** — 6 per side, ~10-15 lb DB
10. **DB front-rack reverse lunge** — 5 per side, ~25-35 lb total

### Phase 3: Glute + bracing activation (2-3 min)
11. **Single-leg glute bridge, left planted** — 8 reps
12. **Bird dog** — 6 per side, 2-sec hold
13. **Banded clamshell, left** — 12 reps

### Phase 4: Squat-specific groove (2 min)
14. **Bodyweight squat** — 8 reps slow, symmetric
15. **Empty bar squat** — 5 reps slow

### Phase 5: Bar ramp
Empty bar × 5 → 40% × 5 → 55% × 5 → 70% × 4 → 80% × 3 → 88% × 2 → working sets.

---

## Squat (Hip-Locked Day / Recovery Mode)

Same structure, but extend Phase 1 and lighten Phase 2:
- Extra 90/90 hip switches (10 per side)
- Longer couch stretch (120 sec left)
- Drop weighted core work to single-leg glute bridges only
- Add 5 min of PRI-style 90/90 hip lift work (see `mobility-protocols.md`)
- Drop top-end working weight by 5-10%

## Bench (Primary Heavy Bench Day)

~15 min.

### Phase 1: T-spine + breathing (2 min)
1. **Cat-cow** — 8 reps
2. **Thread the needle** — 5 per side
3. **Open book stretch** — 5 per side, slow

### Phase 2: Shoulder + left IR work (3-4 min)
4. **Banded shoulder dislocates** — 8 slow reps
5. **Sleeper stretch, left** — 30-45 sec
6. **Cross-body pec minor stretch, left** — 30 sec
7. **Banded internal rotation, left arm** — 12 reps
8. **Wall slides** — 10 reps

### Phase 3: Scap + cuff activation (2-3 min)
9. **Band pull-aparts** — 15 reps
10. **Band face pulls with external rotation** — 12 reps
11. **Scap push-ups** — 8 reps

### Phase 4: Hips + leg drive prep (3 min)
12. **Couch stretch, left** — 60 sec
13. **Couch stretch, right** — 30 sec
14. **Glute bridge** — 10 reps, hard squeeze
15. **Single-leg glute bridge, left planted** — 6 reps
16. **Dead bug** — 6 per side

### Phase 5: Bench-specific ramp
17. **Push-ups with slight elbow flare** — 8-10 reps (elbow-friendly position for tkm)
18. **Empty bar bench** — 8-10 reps slow

Bar ramp: Bar × 10 → 40% × 5 → 55% × 3 → 70% × 2 → 80% × 1 → 88% × 1 → working sets.

---

## Bench (High-Volume Day, e.g., 3×10 at high RPE)

Run the standard bench warmup but:
- Reduce shoulder pre-fatigue: 12 pull-aparts instead of 15, skip the scap push-ups
- Extra warmup set at 70% × 3 to acclimate to higher reps
- Foot stance slightly narrower than usual (reduces adductor demand)
- Leg drive cue: drive heels down, not out

3. Couch stretch, left × 60 sec

## Sumo Deadlift (Paused or Heavy)

Sumo demands left hip extension + IR + external rotation. Tighter warmup focus on hip mobility.

~15 min.

### Phase 1: Hip mobility (5 min)
1. Cat-cow × 8
2. **90/90 hip switches** × 6 per side, slow with end-range hold
3. **Frog stretch** — 60 sec
4. **Adductor rocks** — 8 reps per side
5. Couch stretch, left × 90 sec; right × 30 sec
6. **Cossack squats** — 5 per side, slow

### Phase 2: Glute + core activation (3 min)
7. Glute bridge × 10
8. Single-leg glute bridge, left × 8
9. **Banded clamshell, left** × 12
10. Bird dog × 6 per side
11. Dead bug × 6 per side

### Phase 3: Sumo-specific groove (2 min)
12. **Bodyweight sumo squats** — 8 reps, sink deep
13. **Empty bar paused sumo deadlift** — 5 reps with 2-sec pause

### Phase 4: Bar ramp (with pause if paused day)
Empty × 5 → 40% × 3 → 60% × 2 → 75% × 1 → 85% × 1 → working sets.

## Combined Day (Sumo Deadlift + Bench, Same Session)

When two main lifts share a day, run the deadlift warmup first, then a shorter shoulder-focused transition before bench. Total ~20 min warmup.

After sumo working sets, before bench:
- Band pull-aparts × 15
- Band face pulls × 12
- Scap push-ups × 8
- Push-ups with slight elbow flare × 8

Then bench bar ramp (shorter, since systemically warm): Empty × 8 → 55% × 3 → 70% × 2 → 80% × 1 → working sets.

## Post-Sitting Reset (When Long Sitting Has Cooled You Down)

5 minutes. Run between gym arrival and starting your warmup if you sat for 30+ min before the gym, or between sessions if you've been sitting.

1. Cat-cow × 8 slow
2. Couch stretch, left × 60 sec
3. Glute bridge × 10, hard squeeze
4. Walking / hip circles × 30 sec
5. Band pull-aparts × 15
6. Band face pulls × 12

Then proceed to whatever your session warmup was supposed to be (or directly to bar ramp if you'd already done the warmup before sitting).

## Travel / Hotel Gym Warmup

For when you don't have a barbell or rack — useful before machine-based sessions:

1. Cat-cow × 8
2. World's greatest stretch × 3 per side
3. Couch stretch, left × 60 sec; right × 30 sec
4. Glute bridge × 10
5. Bird dog × 6 per side
6. Push-ups × 8
7. Bodyweight squat × 10
8. Banded pull-apart × 15 (if you brought a band)

## Cooldown Defaults (Post-Lift)

After working sets, ~3 min:
- Couch stretch, left × 60-90 sec (tissue is warm, this hold sticks better)
- Open book stretch × 5 per side
- Cross-body pec stretch, left × 30 sec
- Easy walking 3-5 min if available
