## Week 1 Stop Conditions (Mobility Agent's overrides)

If any of these surface, modify the next session or escalate to Head Coach immediately:
- Left hip pinch/impingement at depth on any squat → cut depth to box height, finish at RPE 6 max; if persists 2 sessions running, swap to pin squat or Hatfield SSB
- Right adductor or right high hamstring lights up → over-loaded/over-cued left; drop working weight 10%, return to symmetric cueing, add extra adductor pullback
- Left SI joint cranky post-session > 24h → skip next hinge variant, sub trap bar at RPE 5, double PRI resets daily
- Recovery feel ≤ 3/10 two sessions running → mandatory volume cut 30% on next session, intensity holds
- Loss of ROM vs Mon baseline on any of the 5 self-assessment tests → pause progression, repeat Wk 1 dosing in Wk 2
- Any sharp left-side pain → stop session, escalate
