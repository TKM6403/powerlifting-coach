## The big picture (read this first)

- **Mobility leads now — your call, and it's the right one.** Your big lifts are gated and in maintenance on purpose. The corrective work is the actual bottleneck and the only thing that reopens the gates, so we make it the main event, not the warmup-you-skip.

The slider is a *test-and-quiet* tool — a correct glide produces ZERO symptom; feel any tickle down the left leg and you STOP. It does **not** open hinging on its own.
- **Sitting is still your #1 nerve trigger.** The hourly anti-sitting beats the lifting for protecting the leg.

## Daily core — EVERY day (home, ~7 min)

Do this every day, training or not. Full how-to in `reference/mobility-protocols.md`.
1. **Daily reset** — 90/90 hip lift (on your back, feet up on a wall/chair, knees & hips ~90°, tailbone an inch off the floor, breathe all the way out) + all-fours belly lift. 2 rounds.
2. **Left-glute wake-up** — single-leg LEFT glute bridge × 10–12, hard squeeze.
3. **Anti-sitting** — every hour seated: stand + 10 glute squeezes or a 30-sec left couch stretch (kneel, left shin against a wall/couch, right foot forward, hips forward, squeeze the left glute).
4. **Ankle** — left peroneal band 2×15 + eyes-closed single-leg balance, left.

**Stop signal (all week, everything):** any shooting/tickling/electric down the left leg → stop that movement, report it. The feeling showing up IS the answer, even if it fades.
