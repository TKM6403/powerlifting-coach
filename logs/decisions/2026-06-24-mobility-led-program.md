# 2026-06-24 — Mobility-Led Program Adopted (athlete-initiated priority shift)

## Trigger
Mid-session 6/24, tkm stated his mobility support had been "lacking" — reactive flags in logs, not a followable program — and that he wants **mobility as the centerpiece** of training (most of his gym time + externals), upper and lower. Prompted in part by the behind-the-back/Apley shoulder asymmetry he self-discovered that day (left can't reach; ties to the documented left lat/shoulder/ribcage restriction driving his bench elbow-flare + rear-delt pinch).

## Process
Head Coach acknowledged the miss (support had been reactive, not programmatic) and spawned the **Mobility Agent** to build a concrete, followable upper + lower mobility-led program under hard constraints: minimum effective dose / 90-min ceiling ([[feedback_program_volume]]); anchor in gym time because rest-day/home work is the documented adherence limiter ([[feedback_rest_day_adherence]]); plain-language athlete-facing ([[feedback_plain_language]]); propose-then-write with cited sources for new moves ([[feedback_movement_proposals]]).

## Mobility Agent proposal (full text in agent return)
Strategy: flip the priority — corrective work is the bottleneck and the only path to reopening the gates (left-leg nerve gate → squat/DL/hinge; left lat/shoulder/ribcage → bench; right quad). Structure: tiny home **daily core** (reset + left-glute + anti-sitting + ankle, ~7 min, 7/7) + gym-anchored **Upper block** (~12 min, 2–3×/wk) + gym-anchored **Lower block** (~13 min, 1–2×/wk), mobility leading every session, lifting as maintenance. Monthly self-screens tied to the gate each governs.

## Two NEW movements + one NEW screen — surfaced to tkm, GREEN-LIT 6/24
1. **Half-kneeling left-arm overhead reach** (left lat/shoulder). Source: PRI left-AIC apical-expansion overhead reach; ribs-down concept per Quinn Henoch / Juggernaut. Low risk.
2. **Sciatic nerve glide / "slider," left** (nerve desensitization). Source: Butler, *The Sensitive Nervous System*; Shacklock, *Clinical Neurodynamics* — slider-vs-tensioner distinction, standard sciatic-nerve rehab. **Framed explicitly as test-and-quiet, NOT push:** a correct glide produces zero symptom; any left-leg tickle → STOP immediately (tensioning, not gliding). Dosed conservatively with the stop signal built in *specifically because of the override history (5/26, 6/9)*. Does NOT open the loaded-hinge gate — that remains a separate planned re-probe.
3. **Behind-the-back / Apley scratch reach** — tracking screen only (the test tkm did 6/24). Source: standard orthopedic shoulder ROM screen.

Everything else in the program was already established (daily reset, glute bridge, adductor pullback, 90/90 switches, lat hang, ribcage breathing, rear-delt work, belt squat, peroneal/balance) — reorganized into the mobility-led layout, not new.
