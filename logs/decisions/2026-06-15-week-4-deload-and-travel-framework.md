## SBD ruling (programming lead)
- **Bench deload:** ~50% volume cut at ~60–70% intensity (SBS/RP/RTS/Prilepin convergence). **Tue 3×5 @ ~70 kg RPE 5–6; Thu 3×3 @ ~72.5 kg RPE 5.** Fast bar, leave 4+ in the tank, **no top singles, no AMRAP, no load creep.** ~24 bench reps/week vs ~50–65 in accumulation.
- **"Take advantage" reconciled:** the barbell is the *tool* to prioritize (comp bench is the only thing that needs it), NOT the *intensity*. Flipping Tue/Thu into heavy days cancels the deload. Stated bluntly for the athlete.
- **Belt squat:** keep at deload dose (don't drop) — only safe loaded leg movement, fatigue-cheap, preserves the squat-ladder rhythm + the gate-screen habit. One session, 2×8 @ ~1 plate/side, RPE 5–6, parallel hard-stop, slump+feeler first. No progression.
- **Hinge/squat/DL HELD** (owed read missing + uncleared gate; deload is not a re-probe week). Keep the daily unloaded leg dose.
- **Back/rear delt:** ~3–4 light sets/week, RPE 6, well short of failure; don't load the pull-up heavy (the one-set fatigue fade is grip+CNS — let it recover; re-evaluate lat-vs-grip in the next accumulation block).
- **6-week template:** ANCHOR sessions = weekday full-gym days (carry comp bench + belt squat + a back movement; any gated barbell progression slots here when cleared, never a travel day). ADAPTIVE sessions = weekends, equipment-driven, **never load-bearing.** Plan each week assuming the weekend yields zero training value; ~2 quality bench sessions/week maintains and progresses (most bench gains live in the first ~2 quality sessions/wk).

## Mobility ruling (owns the travel-risk brief)
- **Headline:** a 6-week travel block is the highest-risk scenario on record for the left quad/nerve symptom — sitting is the documented #1 re-trigger and travel structurally multiplies it. The nerve's fate this block is decided by sitting-management + reset adherence, NOT barbell weight.
- **Non-negotiable daily/travel protocol:** morning 90/90 reset before any sitting; hourly "stand + 10 glute squeezes + left couch/hip-flexor stretch" rule; in-transit specifics (airport walk/stand, plane aisle + get up every 60–90 min, car stop every ~90 min, short toilet sit-time + no Valsalva in deep flexion). Honest minimum if slammed: morning reset + stand-and-stretch out of every vehicle/seat.
