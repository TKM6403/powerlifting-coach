# Decision Log: Pre-Bench "Full-Body Set" — Athlete Feedback (2026-06-06)

**Trigger:** After Fri 6/5 volume bench, tkm: *"by the time i started benching after warmups, my lower body was not ready to use leg drive. i think on any day, we should prioritize getting my full body set, not just my upper when bench."*

**Agents consulted:** SBD Agent, Mobility Agent (parallel). Head Coach synthesis below.

---

## The question
Should we restructure the pre-bench warmup to prime the **full body / leg drive**, not just the upper body — and is that safe right now given (a) the closed L nerve gate and (b) the R-quad strain that just FAILED its 6/5 re-test?

## SBD Agent
- **tkm's insight is correct.** A comp bench is a full-body isometric event: foot pressure/leg drive + arch + upper-back/lat tension + glute brace, all set *before* the bar leaves the rack. Cold lower body → he benches "with his arms," exactly what he reported.
- **No real contradiction with this week's "legs flat, no hard leg drive" call** — he's conflating two things: *dynamic propulsive leg drive* (held out for injury reasons) vs. *isometric whole-body positioning/tension* (safe, and the thing he's actually missing).
- **Proposal (propose-then-write):** after the existing hip reset + shoulder opener, before the first bar warmup —
  1. **Setup-position rehearsal under empty/first warmup bar, 2 sets** — get into full comp position, hold *light static* foot pressure (~30–40%, not a ramping drive), groove 2–3 reps. *Source: Calgary Barbell (Bryce Lewis); Tate / Nuckols (Stronger By Science) — press is a skill rehearsed in warmups.* **This is the high-value piece; keep it if cutting for time.**
  2. *(Optional)* supine glute-bridge hold + lat-tension breathing, ~20–30 sec. *Source: Juggernaut (CWS) / RTS (Tuchscherer).*
- Hard constraint: foot pressure steady + light, NOT a ramping drive. Defers the *dose* (how much arch/pressure) to Mobility.

## Mobility Agent — **RED FLAG on hard leg drive (cannot be overridden)**
- **Hard leg drive + arched setup loads BOTH open files:**
  - **R-quad strain (non-cleared, failed 6/5 re-test):** leg drive is a forceful quad contraction at a bent knee = the exact load/contraction pattern that strained it (confirmed contraction-provoked, not position-provoked, 6/2 + 6/5).
  - **Closed L nerve gate:** a powerlifting arch is sustained lumbar extension; leg drive under a held brace tensions the posterior chain and is a Valsalva event — and the nerve is Valsalva-positive (5/27). Gate has ONE quiet non-loaded reading (6/1), then two zero-PRI high-sitting days (6/3–6/4) with **no symptom data since.** Flying blind → more reason to hold, not less.
- **RED FLAG: hard leg drive + arched bench setup HELD this week.** Reaffirms the program's existing "legs flat, no hard leg drive" call with the injury rationale. Per Hard Rules, Head Coach cannot override.
- **But the complaint is fixable safely** — what he's missing is the *connected, set-as-one-unit feeling*, which is a bracing/positioning problem, not a drive-force problem.
- **Proposal (ONE move, ~90 sec, folded into the existing reset):** "Brace + planted-base primer" — on the bench in press setup, feet planted, glutes/feet to ~25% tension (anchor, don't drive), 2–3 slow **360° brace breaths** ("breathe like someone's about to punch you in the stomach"), carry that connected tension into the empty-bar/ramp warmups. Arch stays flat/neutral. *Source: Nuckols / Stronger By Science + Tate / Juggernaut — stability platform first, propulsive drive layered on later.* Also reinforces the 360° brace he struggles with (wall-slide difficulty 5/27) without spinal extension under load.
- **Progression:** when the nerve gate is open on *loaded* testing AND the quad is pain-free on a graded ramp → progress "stable platform" → actual leg drive in stages.

## Disagreement (surfaced per Hard Rules)
- **SBD** leans toward grooving real leg drive now so it isn't bolted on cold later.
- **Mobility RED-FLAGS** propulsive leg drive until both gates clear.
- **Magnitude:** small — they agree on the *principle* (prime an isometric full-body set, don't dynamically drive) and split only on *dose/timing*. Mobility owns the ceiling by rule.

## Head Coach synthesis & decision
1. **tkm is right, and we're acting on it** — the warmup primed his shoulder but never his base. Fix the *feeling* now.
2. **The two proposals merge cleanly:** rehearse the warmup bar reps *in full setup position* (SBD #1) WHILE holding a 360° brace + light planted-foot/glute tension (Mobility's primer). Drop SBD's optional supine bridge — Mobility's "do it on the bench in setup" keeps it leaner (90-min ceiling, skips-extras adherence). Net: ~90 sec, no new station.
3. **Hard leg drive stays OUT** (Mobility red flag, upheld) — capped foot pressure ~25–30%, arch flat/neutral, explicit stop signals (L-leg shooting / R-thigh pull → back off).
4. **Sequencing:** this is forward-looking. This week's bench days (Tue/Fri) are done; Sun 6/7 is not comp bench. So **propose to tkm for buy-in, then bake into the Week 3 build at the 6/7 check-in** + onto future bench days. Nothing written to `program/this-week.md` for the warmup yet (propose-then-write).
5. **Separate immediate action:** Sunday 6/7's optional lower finisher (leg extension) — R-side pulled given the failed re-test; see `program/this-week.md` Sunday edit + Week Modifications Log.

**Status:** Awaiting tkm buy-in on the merged pre-bench "full-body set" primer before writing it into the plan.
