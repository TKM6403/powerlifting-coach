## Agent positions

### SBD Agent
- 4-week structure: 3 progressing accumulation weeks + Wk 4 realization-lite (RPE 8 top singles for TM recalibration), NOT deload
  - Rationale: athlete entered detrained, doesn't need another deload (Tuchscherer/Pak-Helms "deload is biologically triggered" principle)
- Weekly split: Mon Squat / Tue Bench / Thu Deadlift / Sat Bench-secondary (72hr gap Squat→DL, only Mon-Tue adjacent)
- Variation arc: SSB → SSB → high-bar → low-bar (squat); trap bar → trap bar → conv → conv (DL); tempo → 2ct pause → 1ct pause → comp (bench)
- Wk 1 top sets RPE 6 (below the RPE 7 ceiling permitted) — going below ceiling because chronic 2/10 recovery is a real systemic signal
- Proposed bench TM: 280 lb (1RM 292 minus ~10% post-detrain)
- Effective working TMs Wk 1-3: squat 450, bench 280, conventional DL 450; sumo TM ignored entirely

### Mobility Agent
- Pre-block PRI reset (90/90 Hip Lift + Left Adductor Pullback + All-Fours Belly Lift) before Mon's first session — non-skippable
- Baseline self-assessment battery before Mon (5 tests + L hip IR ROM subjective); re-test end of Wk 1, end of Wk 4
- Daily stabilizer routine restarts at Wk 1-2 level: 2 rounds, light band, regressed Copenhagen — held there Wks 1-2, progresses to 3 rounds Wk 3
- Per-session warmup mapping: Hip-Locked squat warmup Mon (not heavy day version), full PRI reset before Thu DL, daily routine doubles as Sat activation
- Block-specific stop conditions surfaced for Head Coach (left hip pinch, right compensatory flare, SI cranky >24h, recovery ≤3/10 two sessions running, ROM loss)
- Standing recommendation reaffirmed: book PRI-trained PT consult as a block-level deliverable

### Aesthetics Agent
- Wk 1 accessory volume intentionally low (~22 working sets across 4 sessions) given 2/10 recovery
- Weekly volume targets bias toward stated aesthetic priorities: shoulders (2× lateral raise sessions), hams (unilateral leg curl Thu), quads (leg ext Mon), calves (2×/week, alternating standing/seated), arms (hammer curls only — coach's "no random bicep curl" rule), core (weighted knee raise)
- Unilateral-first across the board, left side leads; right matches reps and load, never exceeds
- B-stance RDL Sat as the second hinge exposure (mobility-positive); cable abduction Thu as glute med work (AIC-positive)
- Explicitly NOT prescribing Copenhagen — mobility routine owns it; no double-dose
- No walking lunges, no heavy step-ups (compound the AIC pattern under fatigue)
- Pump finishers held until Wk 3+ if recovery climbs

## Disagreements / tensions

- Conservative Wk 1 load (RPE 6, not 7)
- SSB/front squat as primary squat variant (not low-bar)
- Trap bar over conventional Wks 1-2
- No sumo this block
- Heavy PRI/breathing emphasis
- Daily stabilizer restart at 2 rounds with regressed Copenhagen
- Lateral raises 2×/week as the shoulder priority
- Unilateral-first hams/quads
- Hammer curls only

**Minor tension:** SBD plans Wk 4 as realization-lite (top singles at RPE 8); Mobility's stop conditions could trigger a deload earlier. Resolved by writing both into the plan — SBD's intent is the default, Mobility's stop conditions can override.
