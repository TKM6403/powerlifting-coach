## PT note — why it does NOT clear the DL gate
The PT addressed **left ribcage + left internal rotation** — the *upper* half of the AIC pattern (the bench/shoulder side: L rear delt on breathing, L lat lock, L shoulder ROM). That work is genuinely good and worth continuing. But the deadlift gate is a **different problem in the same family**: the **left-LEG nerve symptom** (tickle/shoot down the L leg/calf) that reproduces in the loaded bend-over-at-the-hips position (slump 5/26, axial SSB 5/26, Valsalva 5/27, DB RDL 6/9). Ribcage/IR correctives improving shoulder mobility is not evidence that the loaded-hinge leg nerve has cleared. Different structure, different provocation, not tested.
