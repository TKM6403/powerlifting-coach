## Mobility Agent's read (summary)
- **L quad at depth:** Pattern-consistent with established left AIC. Left innominate anteriorly tipped → femur more anterior in socket → anterior capsule/rectus compression at depth. Classic "pinch at depth" we've logged before. Manageable.
- **L hamstring shooting tickle:** Character (shooting, intermittent, multi-day) reads neural, not muscular. Mechanism: anterior pelvic tilt tensions proximal hamstring at ischial tuberosity AND puts sciatic nerve on stretch under piriformis. Muscle strain would be dull/localized/contraction-reproducible. Electric/shooting = nerve until proven otherwise.
- **Stop conditions tripped:** "L hip pinch at depth" partial. Not yet sharp pain, ROM loss, or SI flare. State = MODIFY, not STOP.
- **Escalation:** Not today. But if slump test reproduces the hamstring shoot, or it persists past ~2 weeks, book PRI-trained PT.

## Decisions (Head Coach synthesis)
1. **Proceed with Tue squat primary, modified.** Not a full pull. Reintroduction Wk 1 is the wrong week to push through new neural-character symptoms.
